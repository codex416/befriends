# Befriends VPN

**See the world. It's never been easier.**

Befriends VPN is a connectivity service project with documentation for supported clients, setup guidance, FAQs, and public announcements.

- **Website:** [https://交个朋友.com](https://交个朋友.com)
- **Clients:** Clash-compatible clients, Shadowrocket, and NekoBox
- **Node regions:** Hong Kong, Japan, the United States, and Singapore (subject to actual availability)

## Getting started

1. Visit the [official website](https://交个朋友.com).
2. Review the service and account information shown on the website.
3. Choose a compatible client for your device.
4. Obtain your subscription details from your account page and import them into the client.
5. Select an available node and connect.

See the [client guide](docs/clients.md), [FAQ](docs/faq.md), and [announcements](docs/announcements.md).

## Important

Never publish your subscription URL, account credentials, access tokens, or private configuration in this public repository. Service availability and supported regions may change; refer to the official website and the client for current information.
